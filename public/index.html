Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower the youth of Rizal, Laguna through transparent, accountable, and inclusive governance — providing programs, platforms, and opportunities that develop their full potential as active partners in nation-building.

Our Vision

A united federation of empowered, God-loving, and civic-minded young Filipinos who champion good governance, environmental stewardship, and community development across every barangay of Rizal, Laguna.
